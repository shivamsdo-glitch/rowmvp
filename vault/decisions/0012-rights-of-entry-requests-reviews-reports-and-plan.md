# ADR 0012 — Rights of entry, acquisition requests, plan reviews, staff reports and the acquisition plan

**Status:** accepted (Phase 1, slice 3c). Scope: ROW-077, 080, 239, 240, 241. ROW-075 (alignment with the Uniform Act) needs no code: the workflow, deadlines and parties already follow it, and agency specifics are configuration.

## Right of entry (ROW-077)
`RightOfEntry` belongs to a parcel (many per parcel): status (requested, granted, denied, revoked), requested on, **permission given on**, **ends on**, who gave it (free text: the owner or their representative), purpose, notes. Rules (pure, `RightOfEntryRules`): a granted right needs the date it was given; a requested or denied one has none; nothing is dated in the future **where the project is** (ADR 0008); the end cannot precede the grant; the grant cannot precede the request. The database also refuses an end before a grant (check constraint). A granted right whose end date has passed reads **Expired**, worked out when shown (nothing is stored), so it needs no job. The report lists every right on a live parcel with project and parcel, and filters by status (including Expired) and "ending within N days"; each row's dates are counted in its own project's time zone. Rights follow the Parcels page, so every role that reads parcels reads the report.

## Acquisition requests (ROW-080)
`AcquisitionRequest`: who is asking (inside or outside the agency, a name, an optional email), the property wanted, what they want, an optional estimated amount and an optional project. Entering one is a **Projects** create (Project Manager, Admin). Approving or rejecting is an **Approvals** update (Project Manager, Appraiser, Finance, Admin), and **the person who entered it cannot decide it** (403), whatever their role: a two-person rule using the entity's audit creator. A rejection needs a reason. Only the person who entered a request can withdraw it, and only while it waits. A decision is final. Both decisions use an optimistic concurrency token (Postgres `xmin`), so two approvers deciding at once cannot both win. Approval does **not** create a project or a parcel yet: hand-off to project set-up is a later step. Open question: what should approving a request start?

## Plan-review gate (ROW-239)
`ParcelPlanReview`: engineer or surveyor, approved or changes required, date, reviewer (free text) and notes (needed when changes are required). Reviews are kept as a history; **the latest review of each discipline** decides (so a later "changes required" withdraws an earlier approval; ties on a day go to the one recorded last). `AgencySettings.PlanReviewGateEnabled` (default **off**, set by an administrator on the Administration page) holds a parcel at the **Title & survey** step until both disciplines' latest review is an approval. This adds a `StepGate` outcome, `BlockedByPlanReview`, which no override can pass and which also blocks skipping over the step; moving back is never held. Placeholder: which step the gate holds at (`title_survey`) and the two disciplines are assumptions the agency confirms. Recording a review is a Parcels update and removing one a Parcels delete.

## Staff report and certificate of necessity (ROW-240)
`ParcelStaffReport`: a kind, a title and a body (up to 8000 characters, plain text), a status (draft, submitted, approved, rejected) and who submitted and decided it. One **live** report of each kind per parcel: a partial unique index excludes rejected ones, so a rejected report may be replaced (409 `report_exists`, also under a race). Drafts are written (Parcels create), changed and submitted (Parcels update) and may be deleted (Parcels delete); a submitted or approved report is locked; a rejected one is reworked and resubmitted. Deciding is an Approvals update with the same two-person rule and concurrency token as requests. The certificate here is a **text record**, not a generated legal document; the wording and template are the agency's (open question).

## Acquisition plan and schedule (ROW-241)
Not stored. `AcquisitionPlan.Build` (pure) turns a project's live parcels, their milestone dates, its budgets and its latest cost estimate into a schedule: each parcel's next open milestone, due date, overdue flag, planned close (the **Closed** milestone's completion or due date) and progress; the project's first due date and last planned close; and the budget against the estimate (**variance = total budget − current estimate**, none when either is missing, over budget when negative) beside the total authorized. The Plan tab prints (browser print) and exports CSV; text cells beginning `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` are quoted so a spreadsheet will not run them, numbers are left alone. It is read with the Parcels page.

## Shared
Records are visible on a split or combined parcel but take no new ones (409 `parcel_not_active`); another agency's records are not found. The new tables carry tenant and audit columns like all others, and every delete is audited.
